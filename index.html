Request Data Deletion – SupaPanel

SupaPanel is a developer tool for managing Supabase projects. We collect minimal data — only an anonymous device UUID and your Pro subscription status (if applicable). This page explains how to request deletion of that data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to our privacy team and we will process your request within 30 days:

Email: privacy@supapanel.app

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include in your message: the platform you used (Android / iOS) and, optionally, your anonymous Device UUID (found in SupaPanel → Settings → About).

What Data We Hold

  • Anonymous device UUID — a randomly generated identifier created by the app on first install. Contains no personally identifiable information.
  • Subscription record — plan name, expiry date, platform, and purchase token (if you purchased a Pro subscription).

We do not store your name, email address, Supabase Personal Access Token, or any data from your Supabase projects.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your anonymous device UUID and subscription record are permanently deleted from our backend.
  • Any Pro subscription access linked to that device will be revoked.
  • Data stored locally on your device (PAT, project cache) can be removed at any time by going to SupaPanel → Settings → Sign Out, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Subscription transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial and tax regulations. These records are anonymised where possible.

More Information

For full details, see our Privacy Policy. For GDPR (EEA/UK) or CCPA (California) rights, contact privacy@supapanel.app.
